A note of gratitude

ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS

Beyond the Classroom

As this research comes to an end, I would like to take a moment to express my sincere gratitude to MBUS. Ngo Hoang Thong, my lecturer for the International Business course.

Throughout this course, I have gained more than just academic knowledge about international markets, global corporations, and business strategies. I have also had the opportunity to develop a broader perspective on how businesses operate, how decisions shape the world around us, and how important it is to think critically in an increasingly interconnected global economy.

Thank you, Sir, for your dedication, guidance, and for sharing your knowledge and experience with us. Every lesson has contributed, in its own way, to my learning journey. Some lessons have helped me understand theories, while others have encouraged me to look beyond what is written in textbooks, question different perspectives, and connect what I learn with the realities of the business world.

I believe that the value of education is not measured only by what we remember for an examination or complete for an assignment, but also by what remains with us after the classroom experience has ended.

“A meaningful lesson does not end when the class is over; it continues in the way we think, question, and see the world.”

As I complete this research, I recognize that it is not simply the end of an assignment, but also another small step in my journey of learning and personal growth. I sincerely appreciate the time, effort, and knowledge you have invested in teaching us.

Thank you, Sir, for being part of my learning journey.

With sincere appreciation,

Nguyen Pham Minh Nguyet
